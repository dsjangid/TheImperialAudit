# Archival Methodology & Evidentiary Standard

## Evidentiary Grading
1. **GRADE A: Primary Official Record** — Direct government dispatches, parliamentary commissions, and court minutes.
2. **GRADE B: Sworn Testimony & Renunciation** — Eye-witness testimonies and official letters of resignation or protest.
3. **GRADE C: Peer-Reviewed Econometric Model** — Quantitative drain evaluations by established economic historians.

## Econometric Calculations
Drain figures follow the methodology established by Dadabhai Naoroji (1901) and updated with modern compound interest compounding by Prof. Utsa Patnaik (Columbia University Press, 2018), evaluating unrequited commodity export surpluses at a 5% historical commercial rate.
